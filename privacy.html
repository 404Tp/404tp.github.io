Aissistant privacy policy

Aissistant reads one Gmail mailbox, ty.assistant.mail@gmail.com, with read-only access. Its owner, Tyler Pearse, uses it to read sign-in codes and account mail.

Mail data stays on the owner's computer. Aissistant does not send, sell or share any data. It does not change or delete mail.

To remove its access, open your Google Account, go to Security, then Third-party apps, and remove Aissistant.

Contact: ty.assistant.mail@gmail.com
